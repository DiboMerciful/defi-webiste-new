About Deutsch-Freunde Institut

German is the vehicle. Germany is the destination.

DeFI is a Yaoundé institute helping learners connect German-language training with a more informed, personally guided Germany pathway.

A school built around clear next steps.

For many learners, a German course is the beginning of a larger goal—study, vocational training, work, or a future abroad. DeFI brings language instruction, orientation, and human support together under one roof.

Transparent

Honest information and clear limits: no inflated claims, no false guarantees.

Personal

A pathway starts with your background, goal, and current level.

Prepared

Language learning and orientation that help you move forward well-informed.
